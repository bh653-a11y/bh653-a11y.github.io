About

I like problems that don’t come with instructions.

I grew up partly in Kenya, where I lived for 14 years, and adapting to very different environments has been a theme ever since: the Israeli military, a peace-and-innovation NGO, a Series C tech company, and most recently an AI infrastructure marketplace, where I was Head of Business Operations.

Across all of them, the work I enjoy most is the same: walking into something ambiguous, figuring out what is really going on, and bringing people together to move it forward. I’m most at home where people, process, product and data meet.

Now at Cornell Johnson, I’m deepening the strategy side of that toolkit and looking for roles in consulting, strategy, operations and technology where the questions are big and the answers aren’t obvious yet.
